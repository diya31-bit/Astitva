🚨 Emergency steps

1

Call 112 immediately if safe to do so. Leave the line open even if you cannot speak — operators will trace you.

2

Get to a public place — shops, hospitals, police stations, or anywhere with people around.

3

If trapped, signal for help — bang on walls, flash lights, shout near windows or vents.

4

Text your location to a trusted person. Use a code word if you need to be discreet.

5

Stay calm. Breathe slowly. Assess your exit options. Your instincts are valid — trust them.
